I am a third-year Data Science student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), with a strong foundation in machine learning, computer vision, statistical modeling, and programming.

I trained a U-Net model for heart detection and segmentation in X-ray images (over 90% accuracy, 0.7992 F1-score) during a research internship at Seoul National University, and I have conducted research with Prof. Gary Chan at HKUST on making 3D reconstruction more efficient by using 2D floorplans as structural priors. My final-year project explores a personal embodied AI agent driven by reinforcement learning. I have also self-studied financial data analysis and quantitative portfolio modeling.

I am seeking opportunities in data analysis and AI/CV research. Head to the [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) page for an overview of my work — each project card links to a dedicated detail page — or see the [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) page for my full background.
