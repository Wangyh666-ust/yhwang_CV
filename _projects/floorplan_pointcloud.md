**HKUST UROP · Supervisor: Prof. Gary Chan · Sep 2025 – Dec 2025**

Developed a floorplan-guided framework to align and fuse multiple point clouds, using 2D floorplans as structural priors. The approach improves geometric consistency and scale normalization for indoor 3D scenes, with applications to digital twins, robotics, and BIM.
