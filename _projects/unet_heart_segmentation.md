**Seoul National University · SNU–HKUST Sponsored Project · Summer 2025**

- **What:** Automatic heart detection, location prediction, and segmentation in chest X-ray images.
- **How:** Built and trained a U-Net model for the segmentation task.
- **Result:** Over **90% accuracy** and **0.7992 F1 score**.
